# KhetRakshak (Field Guardian) — Hackathon Demo Script

## ⏱️ 3-Minute Winning Demo Walkthrough

### 🎯 1. The Hook & The Problem (0:00 - 0:30)
> *"Over 120 million smallholder farmers in India lose up to 35% of crop yield to late-detected pest outbreaks. Farmers either rely on unverified local chemical shops — leading to toxic pesticide overdose — or struggle with complex apps in English. Meet **KhetRakshak** (Field Guardian) — an AI crop doctor and village outbreak radar built specifically for low-literacy farmers with spotty connectivity."*

---

### 📱 2. Live Demo: Instant Native Diagnosis & Voice (0:30 - 1:15)
1. **Show Language Switcher**: Tap the top right globe and switch to **Hindi (हिन्दी)** or **Telugu (తెలుగు)**. Note how all buttons, instructions, and symptom names adapt seamlessly.
2. **Instant 1-Click Scan**: Tap on **"Tomato Early Blight"** (or snap a live photo).
3. **Show Diagnosis Result**:
   - Primary Disease Name: *Tomato Early Blight (अगेती झुलसा)*
   - Calibrated AI Confidence: *91% Match*
   - Honest Top-3 Alternatives: *Bacterial Spot (8%), Septoria (4%)*
4. **Tap Audio Readout**: Click the **"सुनिए (Listen)"** button. The app speaks the diagnosis and immediate action aloud in the farmer's native dialect!

---

### 🛡️ 3. Safety Guardrail: Curated 3-Tier ICAR Remedies (1:15 - 2:00)
1. Tap **"View Step-by-Step Safe Remedies"**.
2. Highlight the 3-Tier Safety Design:
   - **Tier 1 (Cultural)**: Leaf pruning, morning watering at base.
   - **Tier 2 (Biological)**: Cold-pressed Neem oil (10,000 ppm) + *Trichoderma*.
   - **Tier 3 (Urgent KVK Helpline)**: 1-click dialer to National Kisan Call Centre (`1800-180-1551`) and district KVK officer.
   - *Key takeaway for judges*: **No hallucinatory chemical dosing formulas.**

---

### 📡 4. Village Outbreak Radar & Micro-Climate Intelligence (2:00 - 2:40)
1. Tap **"Outbreak Radar"** on the bottom navigation.
2. Show the rotating radar sweep with village cluster nodes:
   - **Rampur (Varanasi)**: Red (High Outbreak — 5 active tomato blight sightings + 84% humidity fungal trigger).
   - **Kothapalli (Telangana)**: Amber (Cotton leaf curl watch).
   - **Shindewadi (Pune)**: Green (Low risk).
3. Tap on a village to show explainable risk scoring breakdown.

---

### 📴 5. Offline-First Capability (2:40 - 3:00)
1. Toggle to Airplane mode / Offline history tab.
2. Show that previously cached diagnoses and KVK helplines load instantly with 0 bars of internet signal.
3. Conclude: *"KhetRakshak safeguards farmer livelihoods, reduces chemical toxicity, and stops localized outbreaks before they become epidemics."*
