# KhetRakshak (Field Guardian) — Master Implementation Plan
**AI Crop Health & Outbreak Advisory System for Smallholder Farmers**

---

## 🌾 Project Vision
A farmer snaps a photo of a sick plant in their native language → receives instant AI diagnosis, village-level outbreak risk intelligence, and a safe, vetted ICAR-compliant next action step — functioning seamlessly even under low/intermittent rural connectivity.

## 🎯 Key Modules & Implementation Milestones

### 1. Vision Diagnosis Engine (`backend/app/ml/`)
- MobileNet / PlantVillage-calibrated multi-disease classifier.
- Supports 12+ common Indian crop diseases (Tomato Early Blight, Potato Late Blight, Rice Blast, Cotton Leaf Curl, Maize Fall Armyworm, Wheat Rust, Healthy leaves, etc.).
- Returns: Disease Name (scientific + common vernacular), Confidence %, Top 3 honest alternatives, and symptom description.

### 2. Village Outbreak Risk Engine (`backend/app/risk_engine/`)
- **Explainable Rule-Based Scorer**:
  $$\text{Risk Score} = w_1 \cdot N_{\text{recent\_reports\_radius}} + w_2 \cdot f(\text{humidity, rain}) + w_3 \cdot \text{crop\_vulnerability}$$
- Buckets into **Low (Green)**, **Medium (Amber)**, and **High (Red)**.
- Integrates micro-climate factors (high humidity triggers fungal sporulation warnings).

### 3. Safe Advisory Knowledge Base (`backend/app/advisory_kb/`)
- Strict safety guardrails: Never allows arbitrary chemical dosages generated by hallucinations.
- 3-tier safe remedy breakdown:
  1. **Low Risk**: Cultural & Agronomic practices (pruning, spacing, drainage, sanitation).
  2. **Medium Risk**: Biological & Organic remedies (Neem seed kernel extract, *Trichoderma*, yellow sticky traps).
  3. **High Risk**: Extension escalation (Krishi Vigyan Kendra helpline: `1800-180-1551`, local agricultural officer contacts).

### 4. Multilingual & Accessibility Layer (`frontend/src/i18n/`)
- Instant 1-tap switching between Hindi, Telugu, Marathi, Tamil, Kannada, and English.
- Voice-First accessibility: Speech synthesis for low-literacy farmers and speech recognition for verbal symptom descriptions.

### 5. Offline-First PWA (`frontend/src/offline/`)
- IndexedDB & LocalStorage advisory caching.
- Last 10 diagnoses and emergency village guidance available with zero network bars.
